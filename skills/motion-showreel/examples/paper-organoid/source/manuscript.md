# A timed WNT pulse doubles mature enterocytes in human intestinal organoids

*Article · fictional manuscript written for this example · all data simulated (paper-src/simulate.py)*

Ruth Lindqvist¹, Arjun Mehta¹, Sofia Costa² & Yusuf Demir¹ ✉ · ¹Laboratory of Stem Cell Engineering (fictional) · ²Organoid Core

## Abstract
Human intestinal organoids grow from single LGR5⁺ stem cells but rarely mature: most cultures stay crypt-like.
We varied the timing of a single 24-hour WNT pulse across 96-well plates and followed growth by brightfield every
6 hours. A pulse on day 4, after the first crypt buds form, doubles the share of mature villin⁺ enterocytes on day
7 (from 21% to 42%) without lowering stem-cell numbers. The protocol needs no new reagents.
