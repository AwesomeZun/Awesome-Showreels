# OFFGRID: storyboard and build contract

Generated from the project files (reel.config.json, the cut plans, the scene headers and style.json); the scene
headers are the authority for the beat-by-beat choreography.

## 1. Brief

- **Material:** `source/README.md` (readme); `assets/captures/ui/app/` (images); `source/fonts/` (other)
- **Mood:** nocturnal, local, precise, walkable, quiet
- **Purpose:** launch
- **Material:** the app is real (a toyz original, live at https://offgrid.toyz.kr); its places and walks are its own sample data (fictional shop names at approximate Seoul coordinates). Map data and tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), shown on screen as in the app.

## 4. Scenes

### 4.1 `hook`: OFFGRID

- Bars: short 2 · 30 s 2 (min 2, max 2) · in 6 beats, out 1 beats · entrance `cut` · music part `intro`
- Cues: thud @ start+0b, whoosh @ start+1.5b, swish @ outStart+0b

hook (2 bars): from Seoul to an alley. The app's dark map of Seoul with its four neighbourhoods (01 성수 · 02 연남 · 03 을지로 · 04 서촌, each a cluster of lime dots); the camera pushes east and down into Seongsu in one continuous move across the captured zooms while the dots become pins. On the left the app's lime title slab drops in at once: OFFGRID, and the app's line, 관광지 밖에서 찾는 / 나만의 로컬 지도.

### 4.2 `places`: 골목 가게

- Bars: short 1 · 30 s 2 (min 1, max 2) · in 3 beats, out 0.5 beats · entrance `cut` · music part `groove`
- Cues: pop x7 gap=0.25b @ start+0.25b

places (1 bar; 2 in the 30): alley places. The push goes on into Seongsu at street level; the app's seven Seongsu places stand on the map as its own pins and bounce in turn (category icon, dashed for 가고 싶어요, a lime dot for favourites) with their names; 붉은벽돌 커피 lights up as selected and its card (the app's own, captured) slides in on the left. The headline: 골목 가게를 / 지도에 남겨요.

### 4.3 `route`: 산책 코스

- Bars: short 2 · 30 s 3 (min 2, max 3) · in 6 beats, out 1 beats · entrance `cut` · music part `drop`
- Cues: zoom @ start+0b, drawon dur=4b @ start+1.5b

route (2 bars; 3 in the 30): a walk. The camera settles at street level over the app's Seongsu walk (성수 붉은벽돌 골목 한 바퀴, its own ten points); the walk draws itself as the app draws a selected route (dark casing, soft glow, lime line) with a dot walking at its head, the five places on the way lighting as it passes them. On the left the walk's own card (captured): its distance and time count up with the walk to the app's figures, 1.39km and 19분, and its list of places lights in step. The headline: 걷고 싶은 길을 / 코스로 이어요.
