# Haus für Musik

Projektbrief / Project brief
Büro Zwölf Architekten, Zürich
Wettbewerb 2026, 1. Rang / Competition 2026, first prize

Fictional studio and project, demo content. Written for a showreel example; no real site, client or jury.

---

## 01 Aufgabe / Brief

A music school for 600 pupils on a long, narrow plot beside the railway.
24 practice rooms. 2 ensemble rooms. 1 hall with 240 seats.
The building must be quiet inside and simple to build.

## 02 Raster / Grid

One structural grid carries the building.
12 axes along the plot, 7.20 m apart: 79.20 m from axis 1 to axis 12.
3 axes across it: 14.40 m deep.
Every wall, window and row of seats sits on an axis or halfway between two.
One bay between two axes holds one practice room.

## 03 Schnitt / Section

Four floors, 3.60 m each: half the axis spacing.
Levels ±0.00, +3.60, +7.20, +10.80. Roof +14.40.
The hall takes axes 5 to 8 over two floors, from ±0.00 to +7.20.
The two ensemble rooms sit on the hall at +7.20. The library sits on them at +10.80.

## 04 Programm / Programme

| Raum / Room | Anzahl / Count | Lage / Location |
|---|---|---|
| Übungsräume / Practice rooms | 24 | +3.60, +7.20, +10.80; 8 per floor, axes 1–5 and 8–12 |
| Ensembleräume / Ensemble rooms | 2 | +7.20, axes 5–8 |
| Saal / Hall | 1 | ±0.00 to +7.20, axes 5–8, 240 seats |
| Foyer | 1 | ±0.00, axes 1–5 |
| Büro, Lager / Office, store | 1 | ±0.00, axes 8–12 |
| Bibliothek / Library | 1 | +10.80, axes 5–8 |

Gross floor area 4’562 m² (79.20 m × 14.40 m × 4 floors). Plot 1’980 m².

## 05 Haltung / Position

The grid is not decoration. It is how the building is read and how it is built.
Rooms repeat; the hall interrupts the repetition once.
Ein Raster für den Raum. Ein Takt für die Zeit.
A grid for space. A beat for time.
