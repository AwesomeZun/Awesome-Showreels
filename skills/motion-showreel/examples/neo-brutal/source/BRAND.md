# kablok brand notes (v2)

kablok is loud on purpose. Flat colour, thick ink, hard shadows. If a screen looks polite, it isn't kablok yet.

> kablok is fictional. Every creator, handle, price and notification in these notes and on the landing page is made
> up for a motion-showreel example. No real company, no real creators, no real money.

## Voice

- Short. Blunt. Friendly. Talk like a creator, not like a bank.
- Verbs first: Pick. Stack. Ship.
- Periods, not exclamation marks. One "!" per page, max.
- Write the name in lowercase, always, even at the start of a sentence: kablok.
- Say "creators", never "users". Say "page", never "website" or "microsite". Say "blocks", never "widgets".
- Never say: seamless, leverage, synergy, solution, AI-powered, revolutionary, best-in-class, world-class.
- Jokes are allowed. Sarcasm about the reader is not.

## Colour

Flat fills only. No gradients, no glows, no transparency on brand colours.

- Background: paper #FFF5E1 (pages), with lemon, lilac or mint sections
- Ink: #111111 for every border, every shadow and every word
- Primary: lemon #FFE14D for the logo slab and highlights
- Secondary: lilac #C5A3FF
- Tertiary: mint #6FE8B0
- CTA: tomato #FF5A36, the one button that matters (one per screen)
- Card: white #FFFFFF

Text is always ink. Never set text in lemon, lilac, mint or tomato; put ink text on them instead.

## Shape

- Border: 5 px ink, always (3 px on anything under 32 px tall).
- Shadow: hard, 8 px down and right, ink, zero blur. Never blur a shadow.
- Radius: 12 px on cards and buttons, 999 px on pills and stickers. Nothing else.
- Stickers rotate between -8 and +8 degrees. The logo never rotates.
- Grid: 8 px. Gutters 24 px.

## Type

- Typeface: Archivo (variable). Headlines in Black 900 with -2% tracking; the wordmark and huge words in Expanded.
- Text: Archivo 500 and 700.
- Labels, prices and timers: Martian Mono 700, uppercase, +8% tracking.

## Motion

Things land. They don't float.

- Press: a button moves 6 px into its shadow in 80 ms; the release springs back with one overshoot.
- Pop: a new block scales in from 60% with one overshoot in about 180 ms, then stops dead.
- Stack: a dropped block falls (no easing in the air), lands, squashes 6%, and the stack below it thunks.
- Hover: lift 4 px out of the shadow.
- No fades longer than 120 ms. No blur. No parallax. No floaty easing.
- Marquees scroll left at 80 px/s, always.

## Sound

Our launch videos groove: a slap-ish bass line, dry claps on 2 and 4, sixteenth hats with a little swing and a clav
stab on the hits, somewhere around 112 to 116 BPM. Every press is a click, every drop is a thunk. No EDM risers, no
cinematic booms, no "inspiring" piano.

## Logo

- lowercase "kablok" in Archivo Black Expanded, ink, on a lemon slab with a 5 px ink border and an 8 px shadow.
- Clear space: the height of the "k" on every side.
- Never recolour the slab. Never put the logo on a photo or a gradient. Never rotate the logo.
