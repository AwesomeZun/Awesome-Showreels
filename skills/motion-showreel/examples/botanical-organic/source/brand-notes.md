# Mistfold brand notes

These notes keep Mistfold looking and sounding like the hillside it comes from. When in doubt, slow down.

## Essence

One quiet hillside, two rituals: a cup at first light, a balm at dusk. Everything we make comes from the same tea
bushes, grown slowly and picked by hand.

## Voice

- Calm, plain and specific. Short sentences. We say what we did, not what you will feel.
- First person plural ("we pick", "we press"). Sentence case everywhere, headlines included.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji. No superlatives.
- Latin plant names in italics: *Camellia sinensis*.
- Numbers the way a gardener says them: 80 °C, three minutes, since 1962.

### Words we never use

| Never say | Because | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| organic | we are not certified | grown without hurry, picked by hand |
| all-natural, chemical-free, clean | everything is chemistry; the words mean nothing | made from tea seed oil, beeswax and shea |
| detox, superfood, miracle | no plant does that | name the plant and what we did with it |
| anti-ageing, heals, cures, repairs | a balm is a cosmetic, not a medicine | softens, comforts dry skin |
| luxury, premium, exclusive | we are a farm | small batch, by hand |

## Colour

Our colours come from the hillside: oat paper, moss, sage leaves, terracotta pots and the gold of a steeped cup.

- Background: Oat `#F1E9D8` (paper, always)
- Primary colour: Moss `#4F6B3A` (leaves, headlines that need colour, buttons)
- Secondary colour: Sage `#9BAF86` (washes, young leaves, fills; never text)
- Accent colour: Terracotta `#C2673F` (the seal and one detail per page; never body text)
- Text: Loam `#2B2F23`
- Secondary text: Bark `#4F5242`. Small print: Stone `#66654F`
- Gold `#D4A24A`, for tea liquor and oil only

Evening pages turn the paper to Night moss `#1B2318`, with Oat text and a warmer Terracotta `#D9845A`.

## Type

- Primary typeface: Fraunces, with the Soft axis at 100 and Wonk at 0, weight 300 to 450 at display sizes. Never
  bold, never all capitals.
- Plant names and asides: Fraunces Italic, Soft 100.
- Secondary typeface: Alegreya Sans, for labels, small print and the tins. Labels are letter-spaced capitals (+12 %).

## Illustration

Botanical, drawn as if for a field journal: a fine loam ink line, then watercolour washes that pool darker at their
edges and let the oat paper show through. Leaves keep their real serrated margins and alternate up the stem. No
gradients that look digital, no glossy highlights, no outline heavier than a pen nib.

## Motion

Things grow; they do not pop. Stems lengthen, leaves unfurl, ink spreads into the paper, steam drifts. Keep time
with the music the way a picker walks the rows: one leaf per beat, never faster. Ease out softly. No bounce, no
overshoot, no flashes, no hard cuts: one picture bleeds into the next the way wet colour does. Something should
always be moving a little, a leaf in the draught or dust in the light.

## Sound

Acoustic and unhurried, at walking pace: about 96 beats per minute. Fingerpicked nylon-string guitar and a wooden
marimba carry the tune; a soft upright bass and a brushed shaker keep time. No synthesisers, no drum machines, no
whooshes. Our sound logo is three plucked notes rising, like the seal being pressed.

Voice-over: none. Let the garden speak, and set the words on screen, small.

## The seal

The seal is our only mark: a terracotta ring around a sprig of two leaves and a bud, with MISTFOLD across the top.

- Terracotta on Oat, or Oat on Night moss. Never recolour it otherwise.
- Clear space: the height of the bud on every side. Minimum size 18 mm.
- Never rotate, stretch or outline it. Never animate it apart: it is pressed, like a stamp.
