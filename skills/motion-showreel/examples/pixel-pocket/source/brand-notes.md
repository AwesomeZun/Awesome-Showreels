# Brand notes (Ridgeline Pocket)

- **Palette (exactly four):** shadow `#1B2B1A`, moss `#3E5E2E`, lichen `#8FAE3A`, paper `#D4E59A`. Never a fifth colour.
- **Screen:** 240 x 135 pixels, shown at whole-number scale. The pixel grid may show faintly, like an LCD.
- **Type:** the in-house 5x7 bitmap font, uppercase. Numbers are tabular.
- **Tone:** calm, outdoorsy, a little nostalgic, confident. Short lines. No exclamation marks except "SUMMIT!".
- **Motion:** stepped at 9 frames per second; the LCD leaves a faint ghost of the previous step.
- **Never:** neon, glow, smooth zooms, gradients, stock photos of mountains.
