# THE EMERALD FAN · opening-night press kit

For the ladies and gentlemen of the press, with the compliments of Mr. and Mrs. Ambrose Delacourt.

THE EMERALD FAN opens on Saturday, the ninth of October, nineteen hundred and twenty-six, at Number Nine, Meridian
Arcade: a supper club and jazz room behind an emerald door, with a gilded fan above the bandstand and a sunburst
laid in the floor of the dance hall.

## The band

Leo Halloran and His Emerald Seven: trumpet, clarinet, trombone, piano, banjo, string bass and drums. They play hot
and sweet: foxtrots and stomps at an easy, medium tempo for dancing, the string bass walking four to the bar, the
piano comping, the drummer on brushes after midnight. The house tune, *The Meridian Stomp*, is played for the
first time at midnight. Miss Celeste Arden sings at eleven.

## The room

Lacquer black walls, gold-leaf sunbursts, emerald banquettes, and a champagne tower on the bandstand on the
opening night. Supper is served from ten o'clock until two.
