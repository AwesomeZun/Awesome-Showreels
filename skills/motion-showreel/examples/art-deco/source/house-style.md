# THE EMERALD FAN · house style

Notes for the printer, the sign painter and the scenic artist. One look everywhere: the door, the card, the menu
and the bandstand.

## The mark

The fan: seven gold ribs, one for each of the Emerald Seven, opening upward from a single point on the centre
line, with emerald lacquer between the ribs and a gold rim. It stands on lacquer black. Never tilt it, never crop
it, never outline it in another colour, and keep it on the centre line.

## Colours

| Colour | Where it goes | For proofs on screen |
|---|---|---|
| Lacquer black | the ground of everything: the card, the menu, the door frame | #0E0C09 |
| Gold leaf | the name, the rules, the frames, the sunbursts | #C9A24A, burnished to #F3E2A9, shaded to #7A5A1E |
| Emerald | the door, the silk band, the panels between the ribs | #0F6B53, deep #0A3D31 |
| Ivory | small type on black | #F2E8D3 |

Gold on black; ivory for small type; emerald for panels and for the door. Emerald never carries small type.
Nothing else: no silver, no chrome, no pastel tints.

## Type

- Display: **Limelight**, for the name, the headlines and the date. Capitals only, set a little open. Our name is
  set in capitals wherever it appears: THE EMERALD FAN.
- Text: **Josefin Sans**, for details, the menu and the programme. Labels in capitals tracked wide (about a third
  of an em); dishes in upper and lower case.
- Hairline: **Poiret One**, for the lines of the invitation that are spoken rather than announced.

All three faces are under the SIL Open Font License; the files and their licences are in `fonts/`.

## Ornament

- Symmetry about the centre line, always: every ornament has its mirror.
- Sunbursts rise from the middle of the bottom edge; their rays alternate gold and black.
- Frames are stepped like the setbacks of a tower: double gold rules with stepped corners.
- Chevrons, sunbursts and the fan. No curls, vines or flowers; this is not the old Nouveau.
- Gold leaf is laid in squares. The faint seams are welcome: they show it is real.
- Gold rules are drawn, never stamped: thin, unbroken, meeting exactly on the centre line.

## The door

Emerald lacquer, two leaves meeting on the centre line, with the fan inlaid in gold across both leaves, so that it
is whole only while the door is shut. Two brass studs, one on each leaf, take the knocks.

## The bandstand

A shell of stepped gold arches behind the band, lit from below. When the band plays, the arches are lit one after
another, from the inside out, in time.

Between sets the fan curtain closes over the stage: panels of emerald silk on gold ribs, pleated like the mark,
opening upward from the middle of the stage floor until the whole shell is covered. The curtain is scenery, not the
mark: it never carries our name. On the downbeat of the next set it parts on the centre line and folds away into the
wings, one half to each side.
