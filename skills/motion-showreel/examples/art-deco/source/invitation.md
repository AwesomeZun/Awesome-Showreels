# THE EMERALD FAN

*Supper Club & Jazz Room · Number Nine, Meridian Arcade*

---

Mr. and Mrs. Ambrose Delacourt
request the pleasure of your company
at the opening of

## THE EMERALD FAN

on Saturday, the ninth of October,
nineteen hundred and twenty-six,
at nine o'clock in the evening.

Dancing to **Leo Halloran and His Emerald Seven**.
**Miss Celeste Arden** sings.
Supper is served at ten.

*Black tie.*

Present this card at the emerald door.
Knock twice. The word is **Sunburst**.

Kindly reply by the first of October.

---

## The programme for the evening

| Hour | |
|---|---|

| Ten o'clock | Supper is served |
| Eleven o'clock | Miss Celeste Arden sings |
| Midnight | *The Meridian Stomp*, played for the first time anywhere |
| Until the small hours | Dancing |

## A note for the printer and the press

- THE EMERALD FAN is a supper club. Never call it a bar, a saloon or a speakeasy.
- It is an opening. Never call it a party or a launch.
- Write "Black tie". Do not write "formal" or "dress code".
- Our name is always set in capitals: THE EMERALD FAN.
- On the invitation, dates and hours are written in words. Figures belong to the menu.
- Always give the band its full name: Leo Halloran and His Emerald Seven.

---

*THE EMERALD FAN, its proprietors, its band and its menu are fictional. This invitation was written for the
art-deco example of the motion-showreel skill; any likeness to a real club or person is a coincidence.*
