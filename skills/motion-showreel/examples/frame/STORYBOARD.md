# Frame: storyboard and build contract

Generated from the project files (reel.config.json, the cut plans, the scene headers and style.json); the scene
headers are the authority for the beat-by-beat choreography.

## 1. Brief

- **Material:** `source/README.md` (readme); `assets/captures/ui/app/` (images); `source/fonts/` (other)
- **Mood:** crisp, editorial, quick, studio-bright, confident
- **Purpose:** launch

## 4. Scenes

### 4.1 `hook`: Frame.

- Bars: short 1 · 30 s 2 (min 1, max 2) · in 3 beats, out 1 beats · entrance `cut` · music part `intro`
- Cues: snap @ start+0b, click x4 gap=0.06 @ start+0.25b, reveal @ start+1b, tick x4 gap=0.25b @ holdBar+0b, swish @ outStart+0b

hook (1 bar, 2 in the 30): framed. Four orange viewfinder corners fly in from the frame's edges and lock around the centre, where the name lands (Frame, its square orange period dropping in last) with the kicker above and the app's one-liner below: 내 작업을 담는 가장 빠른 포트폴리오. A chrome ball (the portfolio's own avatar image) floats beside it. The corners breathe on each beat of the hold.
