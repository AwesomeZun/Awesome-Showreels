# SOUND: 4 CHANNELS, NO MORE

The ONE CREDIT JAM sound rule in full. Every entry plays through these four voices and nothing else: no samples,
no reverb, no fifth channel "just for the drums".

| channel | waveform | range | typical job |
|---|---|---|---|
| PULSE 1 | square, duty 12.5 % / 25 % / 50 % | C2 to C7 | lead melody, coin and jump blips |
| PULSE 2 | square, duty 12.5 % / 25 % / 50 % | C2 to C7 | harmony, arpeggios (fast broken chords) |
| TRIANGLE | 4-bit stepped triangle | C1 to C6 | bass line, kick-like pitch drops |
| NOISE | 1-bit noise, long and short mode | 16 pitches | snare, hats, explosions, the coin slot |

- Mono mix. Volume per channel in 16 steps (0 to 15). Envelopes are allowed; filters are not.
- Vibrato and pitch slides are allowed (they are just fast pitch changes).
- The engine ticks at **60 Hz**. Notes change on ticks, never between them.

## TEMPO

The jam's attract tune, **INSERT COIN**, runs at **144 BPM**: 25 ticks per beat, 100 ticks per bar, 16th notes on a
6 / 6 / 6 / 7 tick swing that nobody hears. Write at any tempo you like; 144 is the house tempo for the jam page,
the trailer and the playing-week stream.

INSERT COIN is in **D mixolydian**, four bars round: PULSE 1 plays the hook, PULSE 2 arpeggiates D, C and G, the
TRIANGLE walks the bass, the NOISE channel plays kick-snare on 1 and 3 with closed hats on every 8th.

## SOUND EFFECTS EVERY ENTRY SHOULD HAVE

| moment | recipe |
|---|---|
| coin in | PULSE 1, duty 50 %, B5 then E6, 4 ticks each |
| jump | PULSE 1, duty 25 %, pitch slide up an octave over 10 ticks |
| stomp | NOISE short mode, 6 ticks + TRIANGLE drop |
| pick-up | PULSE 2, duty 12.5 %, three-note arpeggio up |
| hit / GAME OVER | NOISE long mode + PULSE 1 sliding down two octaves |
| 1UP / high score | PULSE 1 + PULSE 2 in octaves, eight fast notes up the scale |
