## 4. Scenes

### 4.1 `hook`: PIXEL KIT

- Bars: short 2 · 30 s 2 (min 2, max 2) · in 6 beats, out 0.5 beats · entrance `cut` · music part `intro`
- Cues: pop x4 gap=0.125b @ start+0b, flip x3 gap=0.5b @ start+0.5b, whip @ outStart+0b

hook (2 bars): the name. The app's mark assembles pixel by pixel, PIXEL KIT and the app's line, 작은 브랜드를 위한 이미지 제작 도구, while on the right the three sample kits' portrait designs (the app's own renders) fan out like cards.

### 4.2 `sizes`: 같은 얼굴

- Bars: short 3 · 30 s 4 (min 3, max 4) · in 10 beats, out 1 beats · entrance `whip` · music part `groove`
- Cues: snap x6 gap=0.5b @ start+0.5b, sparkle dur=2b @ start+4b

sizes (3 bars; 4 in the 30): same face, every size. One design (숨결 요가 스튜디오's 10월 저녁 클래스 소개, the app's split layout) is dealt into the six sizes of the app's consistency board one per half beat, each frame the app's own render for that size, its size in the corner. The headline is the board's own heading: 같은 얼굴, / 모든 크기.

### 4.3 `kits`: 킷

- Bars: short 2 · 30 s 3 (min 2, max 3) · in 7 beats, out 1 beats · entrance `whip` · music part `drop`
- Cues: flip x6 gap=0.25b @ start+1b, flip x6 gap=0.25b @ start+5b

kits (2 bars; 3 in the 30): change the kit, everything follows. The wall of six turns over frame by frame (a card flip a sixteenth apart) from 숨결 요가 스튜디오 to 모래알 베이커리 on beat 1 and to PADO Coffee Roasters on beat 5: every size redrawn by the app in the new kit's colours, type and logo, with that kit's own design. The kit's card from the app sits top right. The headline: 킷을 바꾸면 전부 함께 바뀌어요.

### 4.4 `templates`: 템플릿

- Bars: short - · 30 s 4 (min 4, max 4) · in 13 beats, out 1 beats · entrance `cut` · music part `breakdown`
- Cues: flip x13 gap=0.25b @ start+0.25b, tick x8 gap=0.25b @ start+12b

templates (4 bars, the 30 only): thirteen templates. The app's thirteen templates (its own thumbnails, drawn in the 숨결 kit) are dealt onto the table one per quarter beat from the first, in two rows, each landing with a small bounce; on the last bar they light in turn. The headline: 템플릿 13종이 / 비율에 맞춰 알아서.
