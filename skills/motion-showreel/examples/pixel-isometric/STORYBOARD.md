## 4. Scenes

### 4.1 `build`: The shop builds itself

- Bars: short 2 · 30 s 3 (min 2, max 3) · in 7 beats, out 1 beats · entrance `cut` · music part `intro`
- Cues: pop:6 gap=0.5b soft @ start+0b, thud @ start+4b, chime @ start+6b

build (2 bars, 3 in the 30): the shop builds itself like a cosy building game. The floor arrives in a diagonal wave of tiles (beats 0-1), the two back walls rise (1.5-2.5), then each piece drops in on the half beat: windows, counter, espresso machine, roaster, tables, plants, menu board; the sign lands on beat 6 with a chime. The barista steps behind the counter, steam starts; a customer walks in during the 30-s hold.

### 4.2 `inside`: Inside

- Bars: short 2 · 30 s 3 (min 2, max 3) · in 5 beats, out 1 beats · entrance `cut` · music part `groove`
- Cues: swish @ start+0b, bloop @ start+2b, ding soft @ start+4b

inside (2 bars, 3 in the 30): the close-up at 2x. The roaster's drum turns and beans pour from the hopper, the barista works the machine and steam rises from the cups, a customer walks in, the menu board reads FLAT WHITE 4.5. A pixel caption types in on beat 1 (ROASTED IN THE WINDOW), then one on beat 4 (POURED AT THE COUNTER). The camera pans across in whole pixels from the roaster to the counter.

### 4.3 `street`: Elm Street at dusk

- Bars: short 1 · 30 s 2 (min 1, max 2) · in 3 beats, out 1 beats · entrance `cut` · music part `groove`
- Cues: swish @ start+0b, sparkle dur=1 soft @ start+2b

street (1 bar, 2 in the 30): Elm Street at dusk. The shop between the bakery and a house, trees and lamp posts; the palette swaps to its evening ramp a step per beat, the shop's windows glow first, then the neighbours', the lamps come on, two people walk along the pavement, and once it is dark string lights chase along the shop's roof edge.
