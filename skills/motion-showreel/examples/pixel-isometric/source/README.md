# Pebble & Bean · neighbourhood roastery

We roast small batches in the front window and pour them at the counter behind it. Twelve seats, one roaster, a lot
of plants.

## Opening

- **Saturday, 8 am.** 14 Elm Street, on the corner by the bakery.
- First 50 cups are on us.
- The roaster runs every morning from 7 to 9: come and watch the beans turn from green to brown.

## How it feels

Warm, slow, a little playful. Like a cosy building game: you can see everything happening at once, the roaster, the
barista, the people at the window. Soft colours, nothing sharp. Evenings, the windows glow.

## House style

- Pastel palette (16 colours, `palette.txt`), soft shadows drawn as darker tiles, never black.
- Isometric drawings, 2:1 tiles, like a little diorama of the shop.
- Pixel signage with a one-pixel caramel shadow.
- Music in the shop: slow lo-fi beats with old game-console sounds.

> Pebble & Bean is a fictional shop made for this example. Address, prices and opening are demo content.
