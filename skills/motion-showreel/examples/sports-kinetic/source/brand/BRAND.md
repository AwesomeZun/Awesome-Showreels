# VELMORA 42 brand sheet

Race day is loud. The brand is a race clock: a black stage, one signal colour, white numbers that never stop
moving. Everything leans forward.

> Fictional brand for a fictional race (motion-showreel example). Not affiliated with any real event, app or
> sportswear company.

## Colour

Three colours carry the brand. No gradients. No glow. No neon. Flat fields, hard edges.

Primary colour: Signal Orange #FF5A1F. The signal: the split that just landed, the lead runner, the "42".
Background: Track Black #0B0B0B. The stage, always.
Text: Bib White #F5F3EE. Numbers and headlines.

Support:

- Surface: Asphalt #1A1A1A (cards, number plates, the phone screen)
- Lines: Lane Grey #3A3936 (rules, lanes, km ticks; never text)
- Muted text: Kerb Grey #8F8B84 (labels, meta, disclaimers)
- Print only: Signal Deep #C2410C (orange text on white paper)

A full Signal Orange field with black type is the loudest thing we have: use it for the one number that matters.
Bib White fields are for print, never on screen.

Status colours live inside the app only, never in brand art: Go #2FBF71 (live connection), Hold #FFB800 (course
alert), Stop #E8392E (medical stop).

Ahead or behind your plan is never red or green. Ahead is Signal Orange with a minus sign (−0:12). Behind is Bib
White with a plus sign (+0:08). The sign carries the meaning, the colour only points at it.

## Type

- Numbers and display: Barlow Condensed Black Italic, uppercase, tracking −1 %. Big. Then bigger.
- Headlines: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold Italic, uppercase.
- Labels: Barlow Condensed SemiBold, uppercase, tracking +12 %.
- Body: Barlow Medium.
- Numbers are tabular. A running clock never changes width.

Files in `../fonts/` (SIL Open Font License 1.1, unmodified, with `OFL.txt`).

## The lean

Everything leans forward 12°. Stripes, panels, number plates and wipes all lean the same way; the italic type leans
with them at its own 7°. Nothing leans back. Nothing is round.

## Stripes and speed

- Race stripes: three bars (Signal Orange, Bib White, Signal Orange), leaning 12°, moving left to right.
- Speed lines: thin Bib White streaks, 2 to 6 px, running right to left behind anything that moves.
- The runner moves left to right: what is ahead enters from the right, what is behind leaves to the left.

## Motion

- A number never fades in. It lands. When it lands, the frame shakes once, for a quarter of a second.
- Cut on the beat. Hard cuts, whip pans and stripe wipes. No dissolves, no soft blur-ins, no bounce.
- Clocks count like race clocks: every digit real, every digit on time.

## Course screens

The big screens on the course and at the finish run the same system, louder.

- The split ticker: a Track Black band along the bottom edge. Every new split slams in from the right on the beat
  and pushes the others left: mat, split, vs plan. The newest split is Signal Orange.
- The bib wipe: between two screens, race stripes and a Signal Orange field cross from right to left. The field
  carries the bib number in Track Black. It lands on the downbeat, holds for a heartbeat and leaves.
- The stat slam: one number, full width, black on a Signal Orange field. The label sits under it on a Track Black
  plate.
- The finish clock: the result, then every split as a bar. A bar for every kilometre, the plan as one orange line.

## Sound

Race-day sound is drum & bass at 172 to 176 BPM: breakbeats under the splits, a half-time drop for the big numbers,
the start horn, and a timing beep at every mat.

## Voice

- Short. Present tense. Numbers first.
- Talk like the finish-line announcer: loud, exact, never cute.
- No exclamation marks. The numbers are loud enough.
- Words we use: split, mat, pace, plan, bib, gun, kilometre.
- Words we never use: "GPS", "tracking", "location" (the app reads mats, not phones), "AI", "coach", "smart",
  "personal best guaranteed", "world's fastest", "download now".

## Logo

The wordmark is VELMORA 42 set in Barlow Condensed Black Italic, with "42" in Signal Orange and three race stripes
leaning in front of the "V". Clear space: the height of the "4" on every side. Never outline it, never put it on a
photo or a gradient, never recolour the "42".
