# Sunlight for breakfast — notes for a short science explainer

*Written for this example. The numbers are standard textbook values, rounded; where estimates differ, the range is
given.*

## The idea in one line
The energy in your morning toast was made in the Sun's core thousands of years ago, crossed space in 8 minutes and 20
seconds, and was caught by a leaf.

## The journey
1. **The core.** At about 15 million °C, hydrogen nuclei fuse into helium. A little mass becomes energy: the Sun turns
   about 4 million tonnes of matter into energy every second. The energy starts out as gamma rays.
2. **The slow part.** The Sun's interior is so dense that light is absorbed and given off again, each time in a random
   direction: a random walk. It is not the same photon that comes out, but the same energy. Estimates for the time it
   takes to reach the outer layers run from about ten thousand to about 170,000 years. The last stretch (the outer
   third of the Sun) is carried by boiling, rising gas.
3. **The surface.** The visible surface is about 5,500 °C. From here the light is free.
4. **Space.** Light travels about 300,000 km every second; the Earth is about 150 million km away, so the trip takes
   about 8 minutes and 20 seconds. Nothing on the way slows it down.
5. **Not everything the Sun sends is light** (60-second cut). The solar wind, charged particles, takes a few days to
   arrive. Earth's magnetic field steers some toward the poles, where they make the air glow: aurora. Oxygen glows
   green and red.
6. **The sky.** About 30% of the sunlight reaching the Earth is reflected straight back to space by clouds, ice and air.
   Blue light scatters off air molecules more than red light, which is why the sky is blue.
7. **The leaf.** Chlorophyll in the leaf's chloroplasts takes in red and blue light and reflects green, which is why
   leaves look green. With that energy the leaf splits water (releasing oxygen) and builds sugar from carbon dioxide:
   6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂.
8. **The wheat.** The sugar is stored in the grain as starch. A field keeps only about 1% of the sunlight that falls on
   it over a season.
9. **Breakfast.** Grain becomes flour, flour becomes bread. A slice of toast holds about 80 kcal. Your cells burn its
   sugar with oxygen; your brain alone runs on about 20 watts.

## Style
Saturated flat vector shapes with rounded corners and layered depth: a dark violet universe with star fields and soft
glows, a Sun in bands of orange, simple light-and-shadow shading on every round object, bold rounded type. No
characters, no mascots, no names of any channel.
