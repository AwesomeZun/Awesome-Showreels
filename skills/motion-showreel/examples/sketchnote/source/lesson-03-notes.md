# Lesson 3 → Box it. Arrow it. Star it.

facilitator notes · Mara · Visual Notes Lab · Scribblewell Studio
room 2 · Thu 10:00 · 90 min · 8 people · the big board + 1 small board per pair

> Goal → everyone rescues one wall of text into one page: 3 boxes, 2 arrows, 1 star.
> Notes nobody reads → notes people see.

---

## 0:00 welcome (5 min)

- markers out, caps OFF → test on the corner of the board (squeak = good marker)
- quick round: "last meeting you took notes in → did you read them again?"
  - spoiler: nobody did
- put the wall of text (below) on the big board, top left → let it hurt a little
- write over it, big: **Notes nobody reads?** → circle *nobody* in orange

## 0:05 the kit (5 min)

3 markers → 3 jobs. That's the whole system.

- **black** → words. Everything you'd write anyway.
- **blue** → structure. Boxes, arrows, frames. Blue never carries content.
- **orange** → the ONE thing. Stars, underlines, circles. Use it last, use it once.

if everything is orange → nothing is orange!!

eraser → wipe in short zig-zags, never circles (circles smear). The ghost that stays behind is fine → it shows we
changed our minds.

## 0:10 the 3 moves (15 min, demo on the big board)

1. **BOX it** → one idea per box. A box is a promise: "this is one thing".
   - draw the box AFTER the words, not before (boxes that come first are always too small)
   - corners can overshoot. Overshoot = alive.
2. **ARROW it** → arrows say *what leads to what*. Label them if you can.
   - straight arrow → "so" / "then"
   - curvy arrow → "why?" (read it out loud: "why? → because…")
   - double arrow ↔ "depends on"
   - loop arrow ⟲ → "and back again" (feedback, repeats)
3. **STAR it** → one star per page. ONE.
   - star the thing you'd say if someone asked "so what did we decide?"
   - star → circle → underline. Never all three on two things.

draw a star in one stroke → start bottom left, up to the top, down to the right foot, up to the left arm, across to
the right arm, back to the start. 5 lines, no lift. Practise it 10× on the corner.

## 0:25 exercise → rescue a wall of text (15 min)

Hand out the wall below. In pairs: box 3 things, arrow them, star 1. Then redraw it on a fresh board.

```text
Weekly sync, Tue. Talked about the support inbox again.
Customers wait 3 days for a reply, sometimes more, and
nobody knows who is on the inbox today. It is a shared
inbox, so five of us look at it and nobody owns it. Sam
says shared inbox = no owner. Lena asked about a rota.
Idea: one owner per day, on a simple rota. Try it for
two weeks, then check the wait time again. Also the
printer is broken again. Next sync: Tue.
```

what a good rescue looks like →

customers wait 3 days → (why?) → shared inbox = no owner → (so) → ⭐ one owner per day

- the wall stays where it is. Box the 3 phrases right inside it, THEN erase the rest
- small icons help → a clock next to "3 days", two people next to "shared inbox", a lightbulb next to the idea
- "try it for two weeks" → margin note in pen, not a box
- the printer → not this page. Cross it out, smile, move on.
- dot vote at the end: 3 green dots each → they always land on the star :)

## 0:40 swap & steal (10 min)

walk the room → steal one arrow style, one icon, one layout. Write the thief's name next to it :)

## 0:50 one thing for tomorrow (5 min)

everyone writes ONE line on a sticky → "next meeting I will box ___"

→ wrap up: step back, look at the whole big board together. Read the title out loud, all of us:
**Box it. Arrow it. Star it.**

## say this / not this

- say "visual notes" → not "doodles" (in the room, with managers)
- say "clear" → not "pretty", "beautiful" or "perfect"
- say "I can't draw *yet*" → never "I can't draw" / "I'm not an artist"
- say "the ONE thing" → not "highlights" (plural kills it)
