# Visual Notes Lab ✏️

**Notes nobody reads → notes people *see*.**

A five-session workshop for people who take notes in meetings: team leads, PMs, teachers, researchers → anyone who has
ever written a wall of text and never looked at it again. No drawing talent needed. Three markers and a whiteboard.

run by **Scribblewell Studio** · facilitator: Mara Lindqvist

> Scribblewell Studio, the Visual Notes Lab and every person and meeting in these notes are fictional. The example
> notes are made up for the exercises.

## The sessions

| # | Session | You leave with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lines & letters | 5 lines → every icon you'll ever need |
| 2 | People in 10 seconds | star people, speech bubbles, faces |
| 3 | **Box it. Arrow it. Star it.** | any wall of text → one page you can read in 5 seconds |
| 4 | Templates for meetings | a board plan for stand-ups, retros, 1:1s |
| 5 | Notes, live | sketchnote a real talk, then swap boards |

Thursdays 10:00–11:30 · Studio room 2 · 8 seats per session

## Bring your kit

- 3 whiteboard markers → **black**, **blue**, **orange** (bullet tip, not chisel)
  - black → words
  - blue → structure: boxes, arrows, frames
  - orange → the ONE thing: stars, underlines, circles
- 1 eraser (felt block, not your sleeve!)
- that's it. Really. No sketchbook, no apps, no "art supplies".

(There's a green and a red in the studio box → dot voting only. Leave them capped.)

## House rules

- speed > beauty → wobbly lines are fine!!
- one idea per box, one star per page
- never say "I can't draw" → say "I can't draw *yet*"
- we say *visual notes*, not *doodling* (people hear "not serious")
- don't promise *beautiful* notes → promise *clear* ones
- draw WITH people, not FOR them

## How a session runs

warm-up (5 min) → demo on the big board (10) → your turn (15) → swap & steal (10) → one thing to try tomorrow (5)

→ the big board stays up all session. Everything we draw lands on it, left to right, top to bottom. At the end we step
back and look at the whole thing together. That's the best 30 seconds of the day :)
