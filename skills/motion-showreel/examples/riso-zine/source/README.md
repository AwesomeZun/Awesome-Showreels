# PAPER JAM #07: the basement issue

**loud music. cheap paper. no ads.**

> **Fictional example project.** PAPER JAM is a made-up zine, written as source material for the `riso-zine`
> example of the motion-showreel skill. Every band, venue, date, page and price in here is invented. Any likeness to
> a real zine, band or basement is an accident.

ok. it's done. it's OUT!!

issue 07 crawled out of the riso at 3am on a tuesday, smelling like soy ink and burnt toast.
40 pages. 2 inks. 0 ads. stapled by hand on the laundromat folding table (sorry, dee).

## what's inside

- **MOTH CHOIR**: the long interview. they practise in a walk-in freezer. we went in. we came out cold. (p.4)
- **BAD KERNING**: "we know three chords and one of them is wrong." (p.12)
- **SISTER STATIC**: tour diary. 9 shows, 1 van, 0 working wipers. (p.18)
- **DOGEAR**: the centerfold. tear it out. tape it to your fridge. (p.20-21)
- **LUNCHBOX RIOT**: they're 15. they're louder than you. (p.24)
- **THE TIN CANS**: how to record on a 4-track, a guide that is mostly apologies. (p.28)

plus show reviews, a crossword nobody can finish, the classifieds
("WANTED: drummer w/ own car + patience") & the tape. see below!!

## how we print it

two drums, one ink each:

- drum 1: fluoro pink `#FF48B0` (shouting)
- drum 2: federal blue `#3D5588` (talking)
- where they cross you get overprint `#3A1654`, a purple-black (whispering, but loud)
- paper: off-white newsprint `#F2EDE3`, 60gsm, the cheap stuff

nothing lines up. the blue drum sits about 1 mm off and we stopped fixing it in 2024.
every copy is a little different. call it a misprint. we call it the point.

the print files are in here too: `issue07-cover-seps.pdf` (one grayscale page per drum, the way the riso wants
them), a phone scan of copy No. 001 (`issue07-cover-scan.jpg`), the flyer (`flyer.html`) and our two inks as a
palette file (`inks.gpl`). house rules for contributors: [house-style.md](house-style.md).

## words we don't use

this is a zine, not a "brand". please don't call it:

- ~~content~~: it's a zine.
- ~~drop~~: it's an issue. it comes out when it's done.
- ~~merch~~: it's stuff.
- ~~limited edition~~: there are 300 because that's how many we could staple.
- ~~influencer~~: you're a reader. thank you.
