# PAPER JAM house style (for contributors)

how to make your pages look like they belong in PAPER JAM. read it once, then ignore half of it.

## inks

we own two drums. that's the palette. no CMYK, no "can you just add a little yellow".

| drum | ink | screen proof | job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | fluoro pink | #FF48B0 | headlines, stamps, anything that yells |
| 2 | federal blue | #3D5588 | words you have to read: interviews, dates, small print |
| 1 + 2 | overprint | #3A1654 | the darkest thing we can print. use it on purpose |
| none | paper | #F2EDE3 | off-white newsprint. leave plenty of it showing |

- tints are halftones, never opacity: round dots, about 45 lpi. pink screen at 15°, blue at 75°, so they don't moiré.
- want paper-white letters? knock them OUT of a flood.
- pink type on bare paper is hard to read. if it's small, make it blue.
- registration is about 1 mm off and changes from sheet to sheet. design for it: let the inks overlap, never butt
  them edge to edge.
- ink is uneven. big floods come out mottled with tiny white specks. good.

## type

four faces, all SIL Open Font License (files in `fonts/`):

- **Anton**: headlines. tall, condensed, LOUD. all caps.
- **Alfa Slab One**: rubber-stamp lettering. issue numbers, prices, SOLD OUT, No. 001.
- **Courier Prime**: typewriter. interviews, show listings, the boring-but-true stuff. lowercase is fine.
- **Covered By Your Grace**: marker scribbles, arrows, "!!", page numbers.

never more than three on one page. never stretch them. never outline them.

## paste-up

- tear, don't cut. a torn edge shows the white core of the paper. free texture.
- masking tape is fine. staples are better. show them.
- stamps go on crooked (3 to 8 degrees). press hard on one side so they print uneven.
- every copy gets its number stamped by hand on the cover: No. 001 to No. 300.

## our videos (tape trailers, show promos)

- stop-motion only. about 10 to 12 frames a second. move things by hand between frames.
- every frame should look printed: same two inks, same paper, same wobble. re-print, don't re-render.
- things SLAP onto the page: one frame in the air, one frame squashed, then still (ish).
- hard cuts on the beat. no fades. no swooshes. no glow. no 3D. nothing shiny.
- tempo: 160 bpm, downstrokes only, like the fastest song on the tape.
- music only. nobody wants to hear us talk.
