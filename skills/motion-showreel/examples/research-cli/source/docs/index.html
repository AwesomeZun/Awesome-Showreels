How it works

  1. 01

    Fingerprint

    Record the GPU, driver and clocks. Runs with different fingerprints are never compared.

  2. 02

    Pin

    Fix the seed (1234) and the clocks. Warm up 3 times.

  3. 03

    Repeat

    Every kernel at every length, 5 times. Report the median and the spread.

  4. 04

    Report

    One table. results/<run>.json keeps every repeat.
