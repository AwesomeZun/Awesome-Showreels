# Orbit Note: storyboard and build contract

Generated from the project files (reel.config.json, the cut plans, the scene headers and style.json); the scene
headers are the authority for the beat-by-beat choreography.

## 1. Brief

- **Material:** `source/README.md` (readme); `assets/captures/ui/app/` (images); `source/fonts/` (other)
- **Mood:** spacious, luminous, curious, fluid, holographic
- **Purpose:** launch
- **Material:** the app is real (a toyz original, live at https://orbit-note.toyz.kr); the cards and links of the space '생각의 궤도' are the app's own fictional sample data.

## 4. Scenes

### 4.1 `hook`: Orbit Note

- Bars: short 1 · 30 s 2 (min 1, max 2) · in 2 beats, out 1.5 beats · entrance `cut` · music part `intro`
- Cues: swell @ start+0b, shimmer @ start+0b, chime @ start+0.5b, sparkle dur=2b @ holdBar+0b, whoosh dur=1.5b @ outStart+0b

hook (1 bar, 2 in the 30): in orbit. The app's holographic knot turns large at the centre of the blue space while the nine real cards of its sample space revolve around it on a tilted ellipse, the far ones small and soft. The name lands over it (Orbit Note) with the app's one-liner (생각이 연결되는 나만의 노트) by 0.8 s. On the last beat and a half the cards leave their orbit and settle into the app's own layout, the knot slides to its corner and the title fades: the next scene opens on that exact frame.
