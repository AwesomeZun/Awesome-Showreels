# Light-sheet tracking of every cell in the zebrafish embryo reveals an early notochord decision

*Article · fictional manuscript written for this example · all data simulated (paper-src/simulate.py)*

Ines Albrecht¹, Tomás Rivera¹, Hana Sato² & Kofi Mensah¹ ✉ · ¹Institute of Developmental Imaging (fictional) · ²Light Microscopy Facility

## Abstract
We imaged zebrafish embryos from 6 to 24 hours post-fertilisation (hpf) with a multiview light-sheet microscope,
three channels every 90 seconds, and tracked every nucleus through development: 4,812 cells and their 1.9 million
positions. Lineage reconstruction shows that notochord precursors commit about two hours earlier than marker
expression suggests, at 8 hpf, while they still sit among future muscle cells. The dataset and the tracking
pipeline are open.
