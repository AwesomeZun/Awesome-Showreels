# Maison Veyrande: identity notes

For press, partners and the studio. These notes describe how the house appears on paper, on screen and in film.
Edition of July 2026.

## The name

- Always written **MAISON VEYRANDE**, in capitals, letter-spaced at about 0.3 em.
- Never "Veyrande" alone in a title, never "MV" outside the monogram, never in italics.
- The collection's French titles are set in italics: *Les Heures Blanches*, *La Dernière Heure*.

## Colours

Ivory, ink and champagne carry everything. No other colour appears in house communication.

- Background (Ivoire): #F3EEE4. Paper, walls, the page.
- Second paper (Perle): #E8E0D2. Plates and the inside of the lookbook.
- Card (Papier): #FAF7F1. Invitations and cards.
- Text (Encre): #16130F. Never pure black.
- Secondary text (Graphite): #4A443B.
- Captions (Huître): #675F54.
- Primary (Champagne): #C8AD7F. Foil, hairline rules, the monogram. Never a fill behind text.
- Champagne text (Vieil or): #76592E. Champagne words at small sizes.
- Nacre: #E6DCD3. Mother-of-pearl, for embroidery only.

## Typography

- Display: **Bodoni Moda**, the house Didone. Regular weight only, set large, at its finest optical size so that the
  hairlines almost disappear. Italic for quotations and for the collection's French titles.
- Captions and labels: **Jost**, capitals only, tracked +300 (0.3 em), Regular or Medium.
- Numerals: Bodoni Moda lining figures, never bold.
- Never set the display face in bold. Never outline, shadow, stretch or animate a single letter on its own.

Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License; the files are in `fonts/`.

## Layout

- Margins of one twelfth of the page width, at least. A twelve-column grid.
- One image, one line. Leave the rest empty.
- Folios in roman numerals; look numbers in two figures (LOOK 07).
- Hairline rules in champagne, 0.5 pt, centred on the column.

## Images

- The house's croquis: sketches in ink, drawn in long single strokes. They are shown on Perle paper with a wide
  margin, never cropped.
- Photographs in a single warm light (2700 K). No props, no collages, no filters.

## Motion, for films and screens

- Slow. Nothing bounces, nothing flashes, nothing spins.
- Type is revealed from behind a mask, never typed out letter by letter and never scrambled.
- A dissolve lasts at least one beat. Hold the frame longer than feels necessary.
- A fine film grain is part of the picture.

## Sound

Piano, strings and silence, at about eighty beats per minute: the pace of the walk. No drums, no risers, no logo
stings.

## Tone of voice

- Measured, precise, quiet. Short declarative sentences. Third person: "the Maison", "the atelier".
- Never use exclamation marks.
- Words we do not use: luxury, luxurious, exclusive, iconic, must-have, trend, trendy, affordable, sale, deal,
  revolutionary.
- Say "couture", not "fashion line"; "look", not "outfit"; "the atelier", not "the factory"; "the hand", not "the
  workforce".
