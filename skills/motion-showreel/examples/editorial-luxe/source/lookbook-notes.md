# Les Heures Blanches: lookbook notes

MAISON VEYRANDE · HAUTE COUTURE · AUTOMNE–HIVER 2026–2027 · 31 LOOKS

Notes for editors. Captions are written as they appear in the printed lookbook: the look number in two figures, the
name of the look, the materials, then the hours of handwork in the atelier. The croquis for each look are in
`sketches/`.

## Colour story

The collection uses three colours and the light between them.

- **Ivoire**: duchesse satin, silk gazar, silk organza. The colour of the toile before the first cut.
- **Encre**: silk faille, velvet, and the line of the sketch. The night outside the atelier window.
- **Champagne**: silk thread, glass beads, lamplight.
- **Nacre**: mother-of-pearl sequins, only on the closing gown.

## Selected looks

**LOOK 01 · MANTEAU DU SOIR**
Evening coat in ivory duchesse satin with a high, open collar, cut close to the body and falling straight to the floor.
Hand-rolled edges. 96 hours.

**LOOK 07 · ROBE COLONNE**
Column dress in ink silk faille. A single line of champagne thread runs from the left shoulder to the hem. 140 hours.

**LOOK 12 · CAPE DE MINUIT**
Evening cape in ink velvet, lined with pleated ivory organza. The pleats open only when the wearer walks. 210 hours.

**LOOK 19 · TAILLEUR BLANC**
Jacket in ivory wool and silk with a sculpted shoulder, over a long skirt in silk gazar. 120 hours.

**LOOK 31 · LA DERNIÈRE HEURE**
The closing gown. Ivory silk organza, off the shoulder, with a long train. Embroidered by hand with mother-of-pearl
sequins and champagne glass beads along the lines of the first sketch. 1,240 hours, by seventeen hands, over eleven
weeks.

## The show

- Date and place: 6 July 2026, the grand salon of the Maison, Paris.
- Light: a single warm source, as in the atelier at night.
- Music: an original nocturne for piano and string quartet, performed live. About eighty beats per minute.
- The walk: slow and even, one step to each beat. Thirty-one exits, no finale walk.

## Captions and credits

- Captions in capitals, widely spaced. Look names are never translated in captions.
- Hours are the atelier's own count of handwork for each look.
- The croquis are the atelier's working sketches, reproduced at their full height.

*Maison Veyrande is a fictional couture house created for this example. All names, looks, dates and figures are
invented.*
